FAQ10//

Everything you need to know

Do you accept complete beginners?

Absolutely. Around a third of our players start from zero. Beginner groups are small — four players per coach — so everyone gets attention.

What age groups do you train?

From 4 years old to adults of any age. Kids, juniors and adults train in separate programs with their own coaches and schedules.

Do you offer trial sessions?

Yes — your first 60-minute session is free. It includes a short assessment so we can recommend the right program.

Do you help with tournaments?

Our high-performance team plans tournament calendars, travels with players and prepares them for national and ITF junior events.

How many sessions are held per week?

Group programs run 2–5 times a week. Private lessons can be booked any day from 7:00 to 22:00.
